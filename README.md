# BuildDraft: Your Drafting Partner

BuildDraft

Professional Residential Drafting & Permit Drawing Support

BuildDraft is a remote drafting and design support studio specializing in residential permit drawings, drafting production, and technical drawing packages for builders, contractors, developers, and design firms.

With more than 6 years of experience supporting Canadian residential projects remotely, we focus on delivering accurate, permit-ready drawings, responsive communication, fast turnaround times, and highly competitive pricing.

We support projects across Canada and also provide drafting support services for builders, contractors, and design professionals throughout the United States.

Our goal is simple:

Help builders and design firms complete projects faster by providing reliable drafting support they can count on.

How Our Process Works

Step 1 – Initial Contact

Send us:

- Project description

- Existing drawings (if available)

- Sketches

- Site information

- Design requirements

We review the information and provide an estimated price range and expected timeline.

---

Step 2 – Project Approval

Once the project scope is confirmed:

- We agree on pricing

- We define deliverables

- We establish the timeline

Project pricing always depends on:

- Scope of work

- Building size

- Design complexity

- Municipal requirements

- Number of revisions

---

Step 3 – Drafting Phase

We prepare the initial drawing package and submit it for review.

The client reviews the drawings and sends comments, markups, or requested revisions.

Clear written feedback helps keep the project moving quickly and efficiently.

---

Step 4 – Revisions

We update the drawings based on client feedback.

Multiple revision cycles may be completed depending on project requirements.

Our goal is to ensure the final package reflects exactly what the client needs before permit submission.

---

Step 5 – Final Delivery

Once approved:

- Final PDF drawing package is delivered

- Additional formats can be provided upon request

- Ongoing support remains available if permit comments or revisions are required
